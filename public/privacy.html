Africa Sportfishing

Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Africa Sportfishing is the catch log, weigh-in and points app of JHMMC (Jim Holder Memorial Marine Club, Monrovia, Liberia) and the anglers and boats fishing West Africa. This page explains what the app keeps about you, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions: {{contact}}.

The short version

  • We keep what you put in the app, to run the club's catch log, weigh-ins, points and tournaments.
  • Your exact fishing spots are only seen by your own boat's crew.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • You can delete your account and data yourself, any time, in the app.

The app only asks for your location when you log a fish or send a weigh-in, and only uses the camera when you take a photo or video. Both are optional in your phone's settings, though without GPS a logged fish has no spot.

Who sees what

  • Everyone, signed in or not: names (or nicknames), boats, approved weigh-ins with their photo, weight and points, the leaderboard, records and trophy walls, and club announcements.
  • Your boat's crew only: the exact GPS spots of the fish your boat logged, and your boat's full log. Other members only ever see rough areas of about 10 km, with no boat names, on the regional map.
  • Your crew and the club officers: scale videos.
  • The club officers (president, vice-president, secretary, treasurer) and the app's admin: what they need to check weigh-ins, settle disputes and run tournaments and memberships, including where the captain's phone was when a weigh-in was sent. Everything an officer changes is recorded in an activity log with their name.

Services we use

  • Render (render.com) hosts the app and its database, in the United States.
  • Esri provides the satellite map pictures, and Google Fonts the app's typeface. Your phone fetches these directly, so those services see your internet address, as with any website.
  • An email service sends password resets and, to the admin only, nightly backups of the database.
  • Your phone's maker (Google or Apple) delivers notifications if you turn them on.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather and sea forecasts (Sea conditions, and the conditions saved with each fish logged). Our server asks it for a place rounded to about 11 km, never an exact spot, and nothing about you. The chart (currents, water temperature, wind and waves) uses a fixed grid over the coast, the same for everyone.
  • NOAA CoastWatch (the US ocean agency) provides the water colour (chlorophyll) and the water temperature (NASA's MUR analysis, and NOAA's own) from satellites. Our server fetches the same area of sea for everyone; nothing about you is sent.
  • Copernicus Marine Service (the European Union's ocean service) provides the ocean currents, waves and tides (on the fishing chart and Sea conditions). Our server fetches the same area of sea for everyone; nothing about you is sent.
  • Map data on the fishing chart: depths from GEBCO, coastline from GSHHG, rivers and lagoons © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), country borders from geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0), place names from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). These are files the app carries; nothing about you is sent.
  • Open Topo Data gives the depth of the sea from the GEBCO seabed map when you use Depth check. Our server asks for positions rounded to about 100 m, and nothing about you.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. The database is backed up every night and each backup is kept for 14 days, so after you delete your account it is gone from the backups within 14 days too.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to More → Edit profile → Delete my account. This deletes your account, email, password, profile photo, your own posts, and your phone's notifications, straight away. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Two things stay, without your name, because the club's results depend on them: fish already weighed in for a boat (shown as "Former member", so nobody's season points change) and your boat's logged trips, which belong to the boat.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13 to use on their own. Club members without an account, such as children, can be listed by name by the club so their captain can add them to a tournament; ask us and we'll remove any name.

Your rights

You can see and correct most of your information in the app yourself. To get a copy of your data, have something corrected or removed, or ask any question, email {{contact}}.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and important changes are announced in the app.
