Africa Sportfishing

Help

Africa Sportfishing is the catch log, weigh-in and points app of JHMMC (Jim Holder Memorial Marine Club, Monrovia, Liberia) and the anglers and boats fishing West Africa. Can't find your answer here? Email {{contact}} and we'll reply.

Getting started

  • Create an account on the sign-in page with your name, email and a password.
  • Join your boat: tap the invite link your captain sends you, or ask them for the boat's invite code (More → Get on a boat).
  • Register a boat (captains): More → Get on a boat → Register a boat. You become its captain and can invite your crew.

Logging and weighing fish

  • At sea: tap Log fish, the species, then Kept, Released or Lost. The spot and time are saved for your crew only, even with no signal; they send when you're back in range.
  • At the weigh station: the station's weighmaster (or, without one, the captain) registers the day's fish with a photo on the scale. The club secretary checks it, and once approved it counts for points.
  • A mistake in a weigh-in? Ask the secretary: they can correct the weight, species, angler or photo, and every change is recorded.

Your account

  • Forgot your password? Tap “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page and we'll email you a link.
  • Change your name, nickname, photo, units or password: More → Edit profile.
  • Notifications: More → Notifications chooses what your phone tells you about.
  • Delete your account: More → Edit profile → Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what's deleted and what stays.

Privacy

Your exact fishing spots are only seen by your own boat's crew. Read the full privacy policy.
